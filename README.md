# My Favorite Anime: Attack on Titan

> "If you win, you live. If you lose, you die. If you don't fight, you can't win!" – Eren Yeager

## Overview
**Attack on Titan** (Shingeki no Kyojin) is a dark fantasy anime series set in a world where humanity lives inside cities surrounded by enormous walls to protect themselves from gigantic man-eating humanoids known as Titans. It follows *Eren Yeager* and his friends after a Titan breaches their hometown wall.

### Main Characters
1. **Eren Yeager** - *Attack & Founding Titan wielder*
2. **Mikasa Ackerman** - *Elite Scout Regiment soldier*
3. **Armin Arlert** - *Scout Regiment commander & Colossal Titan*
4. **Levi Ackerman** - *Humanity's strongest soldier*

### Key Features of the Series
* **Masterpiece Storytelling:** Intricate plot twists and deep world-building spanning generations.
* **ODM Gear Combat:** Thrilling 3D maneuver equipment flight and action choreography.
* **Legendary Soundtrack:** Epic orchestral scores produced by Hiroyuki Sawano and Kohta Yamamoto.

## Why I Like It
* Unmatched mystery and plot reveals that change the entire scope of the show
* High-stakes battles where no character is ever truly safe
* Incredible character development over multiple seasons
* Deep philosophical themes regarding freedom and war

## Series Information
* **Genre:** Action / Dark Fantasy / Political Thriller
* **Release Year:** 2013
* **Studio:** Wit Studio / MAPPA
